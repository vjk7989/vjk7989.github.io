Support

Num Nam supports adult parents and caregivers using Num Nam - Child Health & Growth Guide.

Contact

Email numnamkids@gmail.com. Include your app version, device model, and a short description of the problem.

Do not email a child's medical notes, report files, passwords, PINs, notification tokens, or other secrets.

Medical safety

Num Nam provides educational organization tools, not emergency or diagnostic care. For urgent health concerns, contact a qualified clinician or local emergency service.

Privacy or deletion

For privacy or account deletion, use the in-app controls where possible or email from the Google account used with Num Nam.

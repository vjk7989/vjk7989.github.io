Delete your Num Nam account

You can request deletion even if you no longer have the app installed.

In the app

  1. Sign in with the Google account used for Num Nam.
  2. Open Profile - Parent account - Delete account.
  3. Reauthenticate with Google, review the consequences, and enter the irreversible confirmation.
  4. Num Nam disables account access immediately and signs the app out. Deletion is normally completed within 30 days.

Without the app

Email numnamkids@gmail.com from the Google account used with Num Nam. Use the subject Num Nam account deletion request. We will verify the request before processing it.

Do not send passwords, child medical records, PINs, or identity documents unless our privacy team explains a secure and necessary verification method.

What is deleted

The request covers the parent profile, child profiles, feeding and care logs, synchronized health records and private health files, community content, saved addresses, cart, notifications, device tokens, and authentication identity.

Placed or processing orders are cancelled where possible. A shipped order may delay final deletion until delivery or cancellation is resolved. Minimized order and tax records may be retained for up to 72 months where legally needed. Pseudonymized security evidence may be retained for up to 12 months.

Questions: numnamkids@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

Effective 28 August 2026. Version privacy-v2. Contact numnamkids@gmail.com.

This policy explains how Numnam, publisher of Num Nam - Child Health & Growth Guide, handles personal information.

Information we handle

  • Parent account information supplied by Google and Supabase, such as account ID, name, email, authentication provider, and security records.
  • Child profiles entered by an adult caregiver, including name or nickname, date of birth, measurements, profile emoji, feeding and care logs, and optional medical-profile details.
  • Nanha Yatra numeric health records synchronize automatically for signed-in parents unless they disable health-record sync in Privacy settings. This can include visits, measurements, clinical notes, and private health documents. The local vault PIN and encryption key are not uploaded.
  • Optional photos attached to parent-entered manual height measurements remain encrypted in the device vault. The photo, filename, local path, metadata, and attachment status are not sent to Supabase or the administrator dashboard.
  • Recipe, blog, weaning, shop, cart, address, order, inbox, and community activity needed to provide those features.
  • Device installation, app version, notification permission, and Firebase Cloud Messaging token needed for selected reminders and administrator announcements.
  • Minimized operational events, errors, moderation actions, and audit records. Analytics and audits are designed not to copy medical text, chat bodies, addresses, credentials, or notification tokens.

Why we use it

We use information to authenticate accounts; provide child-scoped logs and records; synchronize data chosen for cloud use; deliver requested reminders; operate recipes, community, shop, orders, support, safety, moderation, security, and account deletion; and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information and the app contains no advertising SDK in this release.

Health information and consent

The local Nanha Yatra vault remains protected by the parent's device PIN. For signed-in accounts, supported numeric health records synchronize automatically by default so they remain available across the service. Optional manual-height photos stay only in the encrypted device vault. Authorized administrators or clinicians may access cloud health information only while synchronization consent is active, with an assigned permission, a recorded access reason, and an immutable audit event.

You may disable health-record synchronization at any time in Baby Profile - Legal & account privacy. Disabling it stops new uploads and preserves the encrypted local vault. You may separately request deletion of previously synchronized health data; deleting cloud data does not automatically delete the separate encrypted copy kept on your device.

Service providers and transfers

Num Nam uses Google for sign-in, Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and Edge services, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for Android notifications. The administrator dashboard may be hosted by Vercel, and public support, legal pages, and test downloads may be hosted by GitHub. Information may be processed outside your country, subject to applicable safeguards and provider terms.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers needed to operate Num Nam, authorized personnel under access controls, legal authorities when required, or another party in a lawful business transfer. Community posts are visible according to the room's access rules and use the chosen community nickname rather than parent email or child identity.

Retention

Active account data is retained while needed to provide the service. Following a verified deletion request, access is disabled promptly and deletion is normally completed within 30 days. Minimized order and tax records may be retained for up to 72 months where required. Pseudonymized security evidence may be retained for up to 12 months.

Security

Num Nam uses authenticated API boundaries, row-level database controls, private health-document storage, encrypted local Nanha Yatra storage, reason-gated sensitive access, and audit records. No system is perfectly secure; protect your Google account and device and contact us if you suspect unauthorized access.

Adults and children's information

Num Nam is for adult parents and caregivers and is not directed to children. An adult enters and manages child information. Do not allow a child to create an account or submit community content independently.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may request access, correction, export, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, or deletion. You can edit many records in the app, manage notification preferences, withdraw health-cloud consent, and request account deletion. We may verify identity before responding.

Account deletion

Use Profile - Parent account - Delete account in the app, or follow the public deletion page. If you cannot access the app, email numnamkids@gmail.com from the account email.

Changes and contact

Material policy changes will be versioned and communicated in the app or on this public policy page. Questions and privacy requests: numnamkids@gmail.com.
